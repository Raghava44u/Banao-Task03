# Executive Briefing: Material Savings & Nesting Optimization
## Strategic Implementation Plan for Executive Leadership

---

### 1. What is the problem?
Every year, your operations purchase approximately 80,000 tonnes of structural steel. Between 7% and 10% of that steel ends up as scrap—costing your business over **$5.2 million annually in wasted material**. 

On steel plate, waste runs as high as 20% to 25%. While your ERP system (Strumis) has an automated nesting tool, your planning team plans cutting by hand because the automated tool creates unusable offcuts. Because steel is purchased separately for each project, valuable leftover pieces are rarely shared across projects, and many end up buried at the bottom of yard stacks where they rust and are eventually scrapped. Furthermore, when clients change drawings late, you frequently scrap steel without a clear record to bill those costs back to the client.

### 2. What are we proposing?
We propose deploying a **Material Intelligence & Assisted Nesting Co-Pilot (MINC)**. Rather than trying to replace your experienced nesting team with a "black box" computer program, this tool acts as an intelligent assistant for them. It scans cutting requirements across all active projects, groups compatible cuts together to minimize waste, and ensures leftover pieces are cut into large, clean rectangles that can actually be reused. Crucially, it only recommends using a yard leftover if it is easy to reach without delaying the crane.

### 3. Why start here?
Nesting is where your largest material cost decisions are locked in. By fixing nesting and inventory visibility first, we directly attack your biggest cost driver ($5.2M in annual scrap) without requiring disruptive changes to your factory floor or heavy machinery. Success here pays for future improvements in yard storage, trailer loading, and vehicle utilization.

### 4. What will happen first?
We will not disrupt your active production. First, we will run a **Historical Replay Test**: we will take 50 to 100 completed jobs from your past year of records and run them through our system to see how much steel would have been saved compared to the actual cuts made. You will see hard, audited dollar proof of savings before any live cutting is touched.

### 5. What do we need from you?
- **Data:** Exports of 50–100 historical nesting jobs and inventory records from Strumis.
- **Access:** Direct conversations with your Lead Nesting Planner, Yard Supervisor, and Engineering Lead (45 minutes each).
- **Accounting Agreement:** A simple internal rule allowing projects to share steel and credit each other’s budgets fairly.

### 6. How long until the first result?
- **Week 4:** The Historical Replay Test delivers verified proof of savings on your historical jobs.
- **Week 8:** A live 4-week pilot completes on active jobs, demonstrating real scrap reduction on your factory floor.

### 7. How much does it cost?
- **One-Time Implementation:** **$280,000**, structured in milestones tied to performance (30% at kickoff, 40% when the historical savings are proven, 30% upon successful live pilot).
- **Ongoing Support & Software:** **$60,000 / year**.

### 8. What value could it create?
Under our base operating scenario:
- **1,200 tonnes of steel saved annually** ($780,000 in direct material savings).
- **350 tonnes of client-driven revision scrap recovered** ($332,500 billed back to clients).
- **Total Annual Benefit:** **$1,112,500 per year**.
- **Return on Investment:** **227% Year 1 ROI**, paying for itself entirely within **3.7 months**.

### 9. What happens if the pilot does not show enough value?
Our agreement includes a strict **Stop Condition**: if the Week 4 Historical Replay Test does not prove at least a 1.0% absolute reduction in scrap on your actual past jobs, or if your team identifies unresolvable factory constraints, the project stops immediately. You will not spend hundreds of thousands of dollars on software that cannot deliver proven, measurable savings.
